- Persistent: "LOGO" placeholder box top-left inside the safe box (about 140x56); small progress dots bottom (7 dots, current scene highlighted) above y 1500.
- Word-synced caption band (simulated): one line of 3-5 words of the VO in Inter bold, white on a translucent dark pill, centred around y 1380. Use a real word span from that scene's VO.
- Frames (each 1080x1920 PNG, file name sNN_k.png):
  S1: s1_a "BUKAN PANAS." huge, with a simple thermometer icon crossed out; s1_b "CAHAYA." huge in accent, sun rays (straight lines) hitting a tilted panel drawing.
  S2: s2_a cross-section of a cell: top layer labelled "n", bottom "p", a highlighted junction line labelled "sambungan p-n"; yellow wavy photon arrows coming from top. s2_b electrons (blue dots) leaving the junction, moving along an external wire loop to a small lamp that is lit; label "efek fotovoltaik"; headline "CAHAYA -> LISTRIK".
  S3: s3_a split screen: left a PV panel -> lightning bolt icon "LISTRIK"; right a solar thermal collector (row of tubes) -> flame icon "PANAS"; right half dimmed to 40% opacity.
  S4: s4_a a straight wire with arrows all pointing one way, label "ARUS SEARAH (DC)", battery icon. s4_b an "INVERTER" box inserted on the wire, input flat line labelled DC, output sine wave labelled AC, to a simple house icon.
