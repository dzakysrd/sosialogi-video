## Corrections to the original posts

No page could be opened, so every statement below rests on search-result snippets (SNIPPET) or is UNVERIFIED.

1. "Dipanasi sinar matahari" (heated by sunlight), Reel A. The search results describe a PV cell as converting light, not heat: light frees electron-hole pairs at a junction (SNIPPET, digitalocean.wpcompress.com and techniques-ingenieur.fr titles). The same results say output falls as temperature rises, with a negative coefficient (SNIPPET: Emergo Voc about -0.29 to -0.30 %/°C; SLP150S Voc -(80±10) mV/°C). So "heated" is the wrong direction: heat reduces output. Script wording is the user's editorial call.

2. "Rata-rata PSH Jakarta sekitar 4.5 jam" (no source), Reel B. The closest sourced figures are: Indonesia national average 4.8 h (SNIPPET, IESR slide); IESR example using 4.5 h for one rooftop in Tebet, Jakarta Selatan (SNIPPET, same IESR slide; an example, not a Jakarta average). A citing paper gives 4.5–5.0 kWh/m²/day for Jakarta, citing Maulida et al. (2026), which was not found (UNVERIFIED). No BMKG, NASA POWER, Global Solar Atlas, PVGIS or Solargis Jakarta value was verified. Conclusion: the 4.5 figure is plausible but has no verified source for "Jakarta average"; the post should cite a dataset or drop the figure. Decision for the user.

3. "12 V" panels, Reel A. The only real module datasheet with electrical values in the results is a 24 V-nominal module: SLP150S-24U, Vmp 37.05 V, Imp 4.16 A, Voc 42.74 V (SNIPPET, solar-electric.com datasheet). So nominal "12 V" labels do not describe Vmp or Voc. For a 12 V-class module, no Vmp/Imp/Voc/Isc values were seen (UNVERIFIED). The post's "12 V 5 A" is a nominal-class simplification, and the datasheet values above show the working voltage is not 12 V. The series/parallel arithmetic itself (2 in series = double V; 3 in parallel = triple I) matches the vendor snippets (SNIPPET).
